This is a mobile application, runs both on iPhone & Android devices. This actually teaches non-native, intermediate english learners what they need to know: the most important words everyone should now. 

The idea is simple: when a native speaker reading a book or news, she knows almost all the words and their pronunciations. So lets make a list of ~40K most used english words based on books, news, social media, ... and make sure the use knows its meaning and pronunciation.

The app behaves like a combination of Anki, flashcards & Tinder. It shows me a words, I swipe which means:
- Do not show again: I'm sure I know it. Say for easy words like Apple
- I had issues, repeat this later: I kind of know but with wrong pronunciation or had issues remembering the meaning
- I did not know it. Repeat often

On each card I should be able to check the meaning or pronunciation. Say by tapping on a card.

In this program I assume that the user is an adult who is honestly and actively trying to learn. 

In next stages we can show statistics about the usage, show stats about the importance of this word, share usage / unknown words to a servers so we can show / brag / share our progress or the difficulty of a word for others, ...

I want to develop this based on a Design Driven Development and in phases. Create the design documents in a directory and then we will develop based on them. 

start with these phases: 
- think about the idea, ask question of needed and create a baseline understanding of the needs
- finalizing the wordlist based on internet resources or any other method
- decide on the language and tech stack
- make an MPV, ...
